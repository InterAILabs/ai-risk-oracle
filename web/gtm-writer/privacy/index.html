InterAI GTM Writer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how InterAI Labs uses Google account data through its private desktop tool, InterAI GTM Writer. The application is operated for InterAI Labs' own technical research and business outreach, using an account explicitly authorized by its owner.

Google data accessed

Depending on the consent granted, the application may access: Gmail message metadata and content needed to check previous contact, replies, sent messages and delivery errors; data in the designated Google Sheets research queue and CRM; and limited Google Drive file metadata needed to validate the designated spreadsheets.

If the account owner separately enables the outbound function, the application can send business messages through Gmail under a bounded campaign authorization. Email sending is not enabled by merely authorizing read access.

How data is used

Google user data is used to avoid duplicate messages, check business communication history, prepare and record explicitly authorized outreach, reconcile sending status, identify responses or bounced emails, and maintain auditable contact records. It is not sold, used for advertising personalization, or used to train a general-purpose AI model by this desktop application.

Storage and security

The application maintains operational state in a local SQLite database on the authorized Windows device. OAuth credentials are stored locally using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Research and contact status may also be stored in the InterAI Labs Google Sheets CRM and authorized encrypted backups. A business message sent through Gmail is retained in Gmail according to the account's settings.

Sharing and service providers

The desktop application communicates with Google APIs as needed to perform the functions described here. Outbound messages are shared with their intended recipients when the campaign is authorized. The application does not sell Google user data or distribute mailbox contents to data brokers. Access to Google's services is subject to Google's own terms and privacy controls.

Retention, access and deletion

Records are retained for operational audit, duplicate-send prevention and response tracking while the internal outreach activity remains active, or as required for legitimate recordkeeping. The authorized account owner can revoke the application's Google access through Google Account security settings and request deletion of locally stored operational data and associated backups. Revoking access prevents future API operations but does not automatically erase earlier business records or already delivered messages.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is limited to the described user-facing operational functionality, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Any additional use requiring new OAuth permissions must first be authorized by the Google account owner.

Contact

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact interailabs@gmail.com.
