InterAI GTM Writer

InterAI GTM Writer is a private Windows application used by InterAI Labs to manage technical integration research and carefully controlled business outreach for InterAI Risk Oracle.

It is an internal operations tool, not a public product or a Google sign-in service for customers.

What it does

  • Reads authorized Google Sheets research records and commercial contact history.
  • Checks Gmail history to prevent duplicate outreach and identify replies or failed deliveries.
  • Maintains a local operational record of candidate selection and message status.
  • When separately authorized, sends a limited number of business emails from the InterAI Labs account and records their status.

Google account access

The application accesses Google data only through OAuth permissions granted by the InterAI Labs account owner. Its research credential covers Gmail read-only, spreadsheet access and Drive metadata read-only. Email sending requires a separate Google OAuth authorization and explicit campaign approval.

Contact and privacy

For questions about this private application or its handling of Google user data, contact interailabs@gmail.com.
